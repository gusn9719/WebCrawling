### 19:57 - Reduce false mixed labels from absence expressions

#### What changed

- Added protection rules for expressions such as `자극 없이`, `트러블없`, `끈적임없이`, `밀림 없이`, and similar inflected forms.
- Masked those protected expressions before scanning negative keywords so words like `자극`, `트러블`, `끈적`, `밀림`, and `건조` are not counted twice as negative clues.
- Kept the final labels limited to `negative`, `neutral`, and `positive`; `mixed` remains only a rule-based text clue and is not a training class.

#### Why

- Ambiguous analysis showed many `positive + mixed` reviews were caused by simple keyword false positives.
- This is still not a replacement for human labeling; it is a small rule-based correction to reduce obvious label noise before LSTM training.

#### Files touched

- `oliveyoung_crawler/preprocess/config.py` - added protected absence expressions and regex patterns
- `oliveyoung_crawler/preprocess/labeling.py` - masked protected expressions before negative keyword matching
- `oliveyoung_crawler/README.md` - briefly documented the false-positive protection
- `oliveyoung_crawler/preprocess/README.md` - kept preprocessing notes aligned

#### Decisions

- Decision: Reduce clear keyword false positives without adding `mixed` to training labels.
  - Reason: The current step is to keep a clean 3-class baseline.
  - Alternatives considered: Move `mixed` into training or treat it as neutral.
  - Why rejected: Mixed reviews still combine real positive and negative clues, so adding them now would blur the first baseline.
